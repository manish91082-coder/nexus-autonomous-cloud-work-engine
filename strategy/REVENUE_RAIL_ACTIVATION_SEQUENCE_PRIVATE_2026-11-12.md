## A. Revenue categories must remain separate

1. **Domestic digital-product sales:** a buyer purchases a defined downloadable tool/template/guide. Track gross sale, platform fee, gateway fee, GST on fees, refunds/chargebacks and settled INR.
2. **Open-source sponsorship:** voluntary support for a useful public repository. Track one-time and recurring sponsorships separately; do not represent them as product sales.
3. **Creator memberships:** recurring support in exchange for a real publishing/community cadence. Do not promise manual support or ongoing services unless explicitly resourced.
4. **Marketplace software subscriptions:** paid app subscriptions with platform approval and billing integration.
5. **Publishing royalties:** long-cycle royalties from original manuscripts, with quality and IP controls.

## D. No-go rules

- No account creation, identity verification, tax declaration, bank/UPI/Stripe/Payoneer onboarding, legal terms acceptance or public publication without explicit owner approval.
- No paid plans, domain purchases, advertising, paid APIs or hosting while authorized spend is INR 0.
- No sponsor tier that promises personal support, custom coding, guaranteed results or trading returns.
- No mass promotional automation, unsolicited messages, fake stars/reviews or automated Show HN/Reddit posts.
- No revenue forecast until actual settled receipts exist.
- Platform payout timing is not the same as first-sale timing. A fast payout rail does not create buyers.

## E. Mission accounting

Every channel reports:
- Gross customer payments
- Platform fee
- Processor fee
- GST/tax on platform/processor fees where applicable
- Refunds/chargebacks
- FX and bank fees
- Net amount settled in bank
- Settlement date and evidence

Only the last item counts toward realized net receipts.
