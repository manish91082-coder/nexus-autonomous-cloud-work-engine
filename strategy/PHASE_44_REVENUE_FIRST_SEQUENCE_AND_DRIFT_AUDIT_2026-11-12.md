# Phase 44 — Revenue-first sequence and mission drift audit
**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Authority:** MASTER_PLAN_10CR_AUTONOMOUS_REVENUE_ECOSYSTEM_V3.md  
**Decision status:** Internal strategy update only. No public release, account creation, KYC, terms acceptance or spend.

## 1. Mission integrity verdict

The ₹10 crore mission has **not drifted in its end goal**. It remains a diversified, zero-upfront, Internet-only revenue system measured only by verified realized net receipts.

However, the execution sequence has begun to drift toward infrastructure, payout-rail research and open-source readiness. Those are enabling activities, not revenue. The portfolio currently has ₹0 realized net receipts and no validated paying customer. Further broad research or another product build would not be the highest-value next action.

## 2. Re-prioritization decision

### Primary first-cash pilot
**Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk — Getvik Free**

Evidence already present in the canonical repository:
- Candidate scorecard: Getvik 78, ownstreet 74, Topmate 72, Payhip + Razorpay 70. Scores are preliminary, not demand proof.
- Product: Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk v0.3.0, private package and listing draft.
- Differentiation: installment-level cash realization, recruiter commission earned only on collected cash, commission paid/unpaid reconciliation, guarantee claim/replacement events, receivable ageing and client concentration.
- Product QA: Chromium and Firefox browser QA, offline/no-external-network checks, backup/restore and accessibility checks are recorded in the Phase 34/42 evidence chain.
- India payout route: Getvik documents INR checkout and UPI payout; account-level PAN, own-name bank/UPI, payout declaration and legal onboarding remain owner-only.
- Product-specific demand and willingness to pay remain **unproven**. Category-level marketplace sales are not proof for this product.

### Secondary lane
**CSV to Action — open-source adoption / GitHub Sponsors**
- Keep its release package ready, but do not let it displace the faster direct-sale pilot.
- Public standalone release remains blocked until the owner confirms project name, copyright holder and MIT license scope.
- Sponsorship revenue is a longer-horizon outcome and requires actual adoption; no forecast is assigned.

### Hold lane
**Orbit Relay**
- Keep private/experimental until human playtest evidence, platform acceptance and India payout route are established.
- Do not treat its existing prototype or CI as demand evidence.

## 3. Next execution sequence

1. Freeze new product builds and broad revenue-source scans for this cycle.
2. Keep the Getvik listing/package private until the owner completes the platform's identity, payout and legal steps and explicitly approves publication.
3. After approval, run a no-paid-plan, no-ad-spend, bounded 14-day marketplace experiment.
4. Track qualified product-page views, checkout starts if exposed, paid orders, refunds/reversals, withdrawable balance, actual payout, support minutes and net realized INR.
5. Interpret results conservatively:
   - One settled paid order proves a first transaction, not product-market fit.
   - 100 qualified product-page visits with zero paid orders is a predeclared reposition/stop signal, not a universal benchmark.
   - Repeatable demand requires multiple independent buyers and positive net receipts after refunds/fees, with support effort remaining bounded.
6. Only after the first sale and delivery are verified should ownstreet or another independent channel be activated. Avoid parallel launches that dilute measurement.
7. Resume CSV-to-Action public release only as the secondary lane after the primary experiment is live or if the owner explicitly chooses that lane instead.

## 4. Owner-only gates (not delegated to automation)

- Getvik account identity/PAN verification.
- Own-name bank/UPI payout configuration.
- Review and acceptance of platform terms and payout declaration.
- Explicit approval to publish the commercial product listing.
- Any spending or paid plan.
- Separate confirmation of CSV to Action copyright/license scope and public release.

No credentials, PAN, bank details, OTPs or private keys should be sent in chat or committed to GitHub.

## 5. Autonomous execution reality

The GitHub worker is a bounded scheduled queue validator/poller. Provider-backed AI inference is not configured. It must not be described as an always-on autonomous reasoning agent. Continue only bounded, zero-cost scheduled jobs and repository tasks; do not assume unlimited cloud compute.
