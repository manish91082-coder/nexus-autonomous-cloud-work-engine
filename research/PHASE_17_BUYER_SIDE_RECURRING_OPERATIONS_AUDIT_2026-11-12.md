## Channel-level findings
- MSP searches surfaced trend articles, vendors and general hiring pages, not current buyer-issued overflow scopes with rates/payment terms.
- IT consulting/subcontractor searches surfaced white-label suppliers and employment listings, not a verified prime contractor seeking delivery of a named funded work package.
- Accounting outsourcing content confirms a market category, but provider marketing pages are not buyer demand or subcontractor intake. No verified CPA/accounting firm vendor onboarding request with named backlog, rate card and payment terms found.
- The weekly reconciliation listing proves the workflow category exists, but it was closed, saturated and required skills/paid tooling that cannot be asserted without evidence.

## Strategic decision
Do not pivot into bookkeeping/accounting execution. No demonstrated accounting credentials, QuickBooks/AppFolio production track record or financial-data control environment exists in the current evidence. Retain the adjacent non-ledger operational reporting layer as a hypothesis:
- source-file intake and validation;
- duplicate/missing-record detection;
- reconciliation exception register;
- human-reviewed KPI/operations pack;
- source-linked audit trail.
Offer only after a real sample workflow, data ownership/consent, acceptance criteria, rate and funded first milestone are verified. Exclude journal entries, tax advice, statutory accounts and money movement.

## Qualification gates
1. Active buyer request and identifiable legal buyer.
2. Scope, sample input/output and acceptance tests.
3. Explicit right to subcontract and data-processing permission.
4. Verified capability fit and honest portfolio evidence.
5. Budget supports delivery and positive net margin.
6. Advance/escrow/funded milestone or short weekly settlement.
7. Clear IP, confidentiality, privacy, access, liability and change-control terms.
8. India payout route and platform fees known.
9. No upfront paid credits/subscriptions unless buyer-funded and approved.
10. Human approval before proposal, outreach, registration, terms or customer-data access.
