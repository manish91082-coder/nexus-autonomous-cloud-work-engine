## 3. Online revenue portfolio

### Lane A — Self-serve digital products (priority 1)
- Original downloadable software, utilities, templates, datasets, workflow packs, calculators, niche reports and educational/reference products.
- Product families created from one validated core asset and packaged into multiple useful formats.
- Storefront/discovery: Gumroad (India bank payout supported; INR payout; $100 minimum; weekly/monthly/quarterly payout schedules; bank payout typically 2–7 business days after scheduled payout; KYC/tax identity required), itch.io for eligible digital software/assets (payout via PayPal/Payoneer; $5 minimum; tax interview; sales become available after 7 days, possible longer hold), and Amazon KDP for original books/reference guides (India direct deposit supported; royalty payment roughly 60 days after month-end, 90 days for expanded distribution).
- Do not count product listing or gross platform balance as received revenue.

### Lane B — Licensing / royalty assets (priority 2)
- Original vector, illustration, video, audio, code/data/template assets only where platform accepts the asset type and rights are clear.
- Adobe Stock accepts generative AI images/vectors/videos subject to contributor eligibility, rights, mandatory AI labeling and quality rules.
- Shutterstock does not accept contributor-submitted AI-generated content; exclude AI-generated uploads there.
- Build original, high-utility, non-spam assets; automate technical validation, metadata, rights provenance and duplicate detection.

### Lane C — Audience and advertising monetization (priority 3, slower)
- Original niche sites, knowledge portals, newsletters and video channels; revenue from ads and eligible platform creator programs.
- AdSense requires original, high-quality content that attracts an audience and policy compliance.
- YouTube expanded YPP thresholds: 500 subscribers + 3 valid public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 3 million valid Shorts views in 90 days; ad revenue sharing thresholds are higher (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views).
- Do not assume rapid monetization; use original value, not repetitive mass-generated content. Treat audience growth as a compounding asset, not immediate cash.

### Lane D — Affiliate / referral revenue (priority 4)
- Use only legitimate affiliate programs with India-compatible payout, transparent disclosures, approved traffic sources and self-serve inbound discovery.
- No cold outreach, unsolicited DMs, spam, fake reviews, cookie stuffing, forced clicks or paid traffic.
- Require a real conversion path and current program eligibility before investing build effort.

### Lane E — Small software / API / subscription products (priority 5)
- Browser-based utilities, data transformation tools, niche calculators, API endpoints, automation templates and lightweight SaaS with free-tier infrastructure.
- Prefer self-serve onboarding, free tier, automated billing through approved processors and no bespoke customer delivery.
- Do not assume zero-cost hosting or payments: verify quotas, limits, terms, fees, abuse controls and payout eligibility before launch.
- No production secrets or paid infrastructure without explicit approval.

### Lane F — Platform-native digital publishing
- Original books, code, game assets, small games, browser extensions and educational resources on marketplaces with verified India payout.
- Validate eligibility, payout method, fees, content policy, discoverability and expected review time per platform before production.

## 5. Execution architecture

Build one shared engine with modular channel adapters:
1. Demand radar: identify keyword/search demand, marketplace gaps, seasonal demand, competitor saturation and buyer intent from public sources.
2. Opportunity score: rank by demand evidence, competition, production effort, policy fit, ownership/IP, zero-cost feasibility, payout eligibility, payout delay and expected net margin. No fabricated conversion probabilities.
3. Asset factory: create one high-value core asset and derive appropriate formats (web tool, downloadable file, guide, dataset, video, images) only when each format has real user value.
4. Automated QA: factual checks, test suites, accessibility, file integrity, originality, duplication, rights/provenance, metadata and platform-specific compliance.
5. Publishing adapters: platform-specific submission only after user-approved account access and any mandatory owner attestation. Do not automate around CAPTCHA, KYC or platform anti-abuse controls.
6. Inbound distribution: SEO, structured metadata, legitimate social/platform discovery, cross-linking and product-led free utilities. No cold outreach or spam.
7. Analytics and optimization: impressions, clicks, conversion, refunds, chargebacks, platform fees, payout status, net bank receipts.
8. Finance ledger: separate views for gross sales, pending balances, approved royalties, scheduled payouts, settled bank receipts, fees, refunds and net cash.

Use free/open-source/local tools and GitHub Actions/Pages or verified free tiers where policy and quotas permit. Never rely on one provider. Do not generate content at scale merely to flood marketplaces.

## 6. Revenue truth and feasibility

Do not use fictitious revenue projections, fake balances, or treat a listing, view, download, sale pending, invoice or platform balance as cleared cash.

Track separately:

- Refund-adjusted platform balance

- Bank settled gross
- Net bank receipts after fees/refunds/FX/other costs
