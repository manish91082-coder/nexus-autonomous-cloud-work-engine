# Phase 42 Staffing-Tool Saturation Audit

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Task:** P42-006  
**Decision:** NO-GO for a generic staffing calculator, bill-rate calculator, DSO calculator or invoice-ageing tracker as a standalone paid product.

## Evidence found

| Existing product/tool | What is already offered | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| alluvo free staffing tools | Assignment contribution margin, billing rate, bench cost and payment-term cash-flow calculators; no signup and browser-side calculations | Generic staffing calculator is already commoditised |
| Vayora agency calculators | Placement fee/rebate, contract margin/markup and recruiter commission calculators | Recruitment-specific commission calculator is not novel |
| Ascent ATS free tools | Placement fee and commission, C2H daily margin, recruitment cost per hire and sourcing tools | Free lead magnets already cover the most obvious recruitment calculations |
| ZeniaHR | Free for first 50 workers; attendance-to-invoice, timesheet approvals, margin per contract and staffing compliance | A new small staffing ERP would face a free incumbent |
| Tagaada | Free browser-only MSME delayed-payment interest, DSO and payment reminder tools | MSME Ops Desk Lite's receivables/reminder wedge is crowded |
| Lekhiva | Free browser-only invoice payment tracker with partial payments, overdue days and CSV export | Generic invoice tracker is already available at no sign-up |
| AiSevak | Paid staffing cash-flow projection tool at ₹99, including payroll-before-collection gap | There is a monetized niche signal, but not proof that another generic calculator will sell |

## Community signal
A November 2025 recruiting discussion asks how agencies track commissions; replies mention spreadsheets as common and sufficient for some teams, while another participant describes automated placement/commission tracking. A March 2026 thread likewise describes agency commission sheets and spreadsheets as common. These are anecdotal posts, not representative market research:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/recruiting/comments/1om5wv0/commission_tracking_best_tool/
- https://www.reddit.com/r/Recruitment/comments/1roslnt/new_to_agency_recruitment_and_im_lost_on/

## Decision
- Do not build a generic staffing margin calculator, commission calculator, DSO calculator, invoice tracker or another generic MSME operations dashboard.
- Keep MSME Ops Desk Lite as a possible free sample/lead magnet only; no paid listing or feature expansion.
- Keep Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk in PRIVATE_VALIDATION_HOLD; its only defensible hypothesis remains a narrow offline financial-control layer, and it still requires real owner validation.
- Do not confuse existence of free tools or a ₹99 paid calculator with proof of our own buyer demand.

## Next action
P43-001: audit existing assets and reusable IP across the portfolio. Select a pilot only where differentiated buyer demand, organic discovery, India payout eligibility and positive net economics can be evidenced. No new build until the audit selects a viable wedge.
