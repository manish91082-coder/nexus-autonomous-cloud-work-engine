## Strategic learning
1. Real business demand is strongest around lead capture, CRM cleanup, routing, WhatsApp conversations, quote/booking synchronization, reporting and human handoff.
2. Generic chatbot/n8n offers are commoditized and attract 20–50+ proposals.
3. The more defensible wedge is a complete revenue-operations automation: lead-source integrity -> deduplication -> qualification -> routing -> approved reply -> quote/order status -> follow-up stop conditions -> manager dashboard -> audit trail.
4. Existing user strengths in ERP, Nexus orchestration, WMCAS WhatsApp/mail automation and operational dashboards can be used to create a truthful portfolio demo. Do not claim production customer deployments that do not exist.
5. Public proposals, account creation, outreach and legal acceptance remain approval-gated. No user credentials or payment details were used.
