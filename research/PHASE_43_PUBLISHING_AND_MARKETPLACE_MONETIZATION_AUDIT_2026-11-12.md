# Phase 43 — Publishing and Developer Marketplace Monetization Audit

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Task:** P43-003 completed  
**Decision:** KDP remains a P2 long-horizon channel, not a fast-revenue lane. Do not create a generic AI automation ebook or publish low-value AI-generated content.

## Amazon KDP verified terms
- India supports direct deposit in INR for Amazon.in and multiple international marketplaces. KDP says royalties are paid approximately 60 days after the end of the month in which sales are reported; India bank transfers may take another 7–10 business days to appear.
- eBook royalties are 35% or 70%. For India sales, 70% requires KDP Select enrollment and the applicable list-price requirements; otherwise 35% applies. Public-domain-heavy books are only eligible for 35% royalties.
- Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Marathi and Gujarati eBooks are supported; paperback publishing in Indian languages is not currently supported.
- KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text, images and translations at publication/update. AI-assisted editing does not require disclosure. Authors remain responsible for quality and rights.
- KDP content policy rejects low-quality, repetitive, misleading, infringing and undifferentiated public-domain material.

## Market saturation snapshot
Publicly indexed Indian AI/automation digital guides and ebooks were observed from ₹49 to ₹1,799, including AI automation playbooks, n8n/AI guides, prompt kits and digital-product starter kits. These public listings prove supply and price points, not unit sales or profitable demand. A generic “AI automation guide” would enter a crowded low-price market.

Examples:
- ₹1,799 AI Automation Playbook: https://market.digitalitup.co.in/products/ebook-ai-automation-playbook
- ₹99–₹1,299 AI guides/workbooks: https://www.parikshitkhanna.com/post/best-10-ai-books-2026-new-releases
- ₹149 AI Agents & Automation playbook: https://digitalkartai.shop/products/
- ₹99 digital product/AI business guide: https://merapatra.in/

## Decision
Keep KDP as a possible compounding channel only if a genuinely original, technically deep and India-specific manuscript can be supported by real reader intent. Do not publish a generic AI book, public-domain repackaging or repetitive generated content. No manuscript was started, no account created and no KYC/tax/payout setup occurred.
