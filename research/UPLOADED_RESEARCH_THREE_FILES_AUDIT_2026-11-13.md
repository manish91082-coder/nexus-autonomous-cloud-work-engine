## 3. Current first-cash platform: Getvik

Seven current official pages were reviewed: homepage, pricing, Terms (effective 9-Nov-2026), Privacy Policy (effective 22-Sep-2026), Instagram AutoDM, About and Discover.

Ground findings:
- Free plan is ₹0/month; public pricing says 10% platform fee + GST, with payment-gateway charges extra.
- Digital product/course access is automatically delivered after successful payment.
- Payout is not unattended: creator requests payout after settled balance reaches ₹100; public page states usually 2–3 business days. Holds/reviews remain possible.
- Before publishing, creator must provide PAN, legal name, own-name UPI ID and bank details, and accept the payout declaration.
- Creator retains responsibility for intellectual-property rights, accurate listing/refund terms, reasonable buyer support, disputes and chargebacks.
- Getvik prohibits low-effort, mass-produced AI-generated content.
- AutoDM uses Meta's official Instagram API, is triggered by eligible comments/messages and cannot cold-DM people who never reached out. Free tier displays 2,000 people/month and three active automations. This converts inbound interest; it does not create demand.
- Getvik's About page explicitly says a storefront does not create demand on its own. Discover counters are platform-displayed activity signals, not independently audited settlements or proof of demand for our product.

**Material fee-document conflict:** Newer Terms say the 10% platform fee is calculated on buyer-paid sale amount after discount, with 18% GST on that fee and gateway charges borne by the creator. The older Privacy Policy says the 10% commission is applied after gateway fees. This discrepancy is unresolved; Getvik support or the seller dashboard must clarify before a final margin is asserted.

Illustration only: on a ₹499 buyer-paid sale, using the newer Terms' base, 10% fee is ₹49.90 and GST on that fee is ₹8.98, leaving ₹440.12 before Cashfree charges, affiliate commission or refund/chargeback effects. This is not a payout quote or sales forecast.
