# Phase 29 — Broad Revenue Model & Payout Evidence Scan
**Research snapshot:** 2026-11-12  
**Mission:** ₹10 Cr autonomous revenue ecosystem  
**Method:** Official platform help/docs and Indian government sources first. This is an initial source audit, not a forecast and not proof of account eligibility, sales, or payout.

## Executive decision

The universe is expanded: one-time owner setup is permitted; recurring human labour is not. Bank, UPI, international wallets, platform balances and compliant crypto-wallet receipts are accepted as candidate payment rails. The highest-priority candidates are those with platform-native discovery, a low enough payout threshold, known India settlement and reusable automated production. No platform is guaranteed to produce demand or sales.

**Current actual receipts: ₹0. Authorized spend: ₹0.**

## What the payout evidence tells us

- **Fastest threshold is not necessarily fastest cash.** Adobe's USD 25 minimum is low, but the 45-day first-sale wait starts only after the first sale. KDP EFT has no platform threshold, but the normal payout lag is roughly 60 days after month-end. Gumroad's $100 minimum and 7-day hold are clearer, but sales discovery is not guaranteed.
- **Envato and Google platform payouts** have predictable monthly cycles, but India-specific account setup, admission and settlement need to be confirmed.
- **Affiliate** is a portfolio lane, not an instant-cash promise: Amazon India NEFT needs ₹1,000 and has about 60-day settlement lag.
- **Crypto is an accepted candidate rail, not a shortcut around compliance.** The Income Tax Department and FIU-IND publish VDA rules. Provider/counterparty status, wallet control, tax records, valuation and lawful conversion must be checked before activation.
