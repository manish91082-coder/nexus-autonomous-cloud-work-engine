## Anti-drift diagnosis
The prior funnel had become too marketplace-centric and too concentrated in CRM/AI automation. That is not yet a proven repeatable revenue engine. Product demos remain synthetic and do not count as client deployments or portfolio proof.

## Portfolio architecture from this phase
Maintain distinct revenue paths; do not force all work into one product:
1. **Direct fixed-scope implementation contracts:** narrowly scoped business process/data automation with paid discovery and milestone funding.
2. **Recurring managed operations:** monthly reporting, reconciliation, exception handling and workflow assurance only where a buyer confirms recurring pain and data access.
3. **Expert/contract assignments:** selective paid technical/research/reporting assignments when credentials and eligibility are honestly evidenced; not job offers or contract-to-hire.
4. **Institutional procurement:** GeM/eProcurement tenders for suitable service categories after eligibility and cash-flow audit.
5. **Reusable software/IP:** build only after repeated paid demand or a funded pilot validates a specific workflow.
6. **Strategic partnerships/channel delivery:** partner with established vendors who already have buyer access and required credentials; define revenue share, delivery ownership and payment protections contractually.

## Mandatory opportunity gates
- Buyer and actual decision-maker identifiable.
- Listing/tender open and independently rechecked.
- Contract vs employment classification explicit.
- Scope, acceptance criteria, delivery capacity and change control bounded.
- Payment terms, payout route and fees verified.
- Upfront milestone/escrow/creditworthiness adequate for exposure.
- Positive expected net contribution after all known costs.
- No false portfolio, credentials, case studies or production claims.
- No submission, outreach, registration, terms acceptance, KYC, payment or signature without explicit user approval.
