## Channel lesson
A "Become Partner" page often means reseller/lead referral, not subcontractor delivery. A white-label services page usually means that company sells fulfillment to agencies, not that it is seeking a subcontractor. We must classify each route before adding it to the cash pipeline.

## Qualification gates for any future partner
Require all of:
1. Named contracting legal entity and authorized signatory.
2. Actual customer project / backlog with client consent to subcontract.
3. Written subcontracting permission in the prime contract where applicable.
4. Specific deliverables, scope, acceptance criteria, deadlines and named reviewer.
5. Fixed fee or rate card, taxes/withholding treatment and currency.
6. Milestone or weekly payment schedule, preferably advance or funded milestone; no open-ended pay-when-paid without protection.
7. IP ownership/licensing, confidentiality, data protection, security and non-solicitation terms.
8. Access limited to client-controlled accounts; no password sharing in chat.
9. Dispute, change-request, termination and late-payment clauses.
10. Positive net margin after delivery, tools, tax/fees and payment delay risk.

## Strategic decision
No verified partner overflow assignment found in this scan. Do not manufacture opportunity from partner marketing pages. Continue the search in a narrower way: agencies/IT consultancies with active public client projects and explicit vendor/subcontractor request, or buyer-side fixed-scope work that can be fulfilled by a disclosed delivery partner. Avoid reseller-only routes unless they create inbound customers without upfront fees or quota obligations.
