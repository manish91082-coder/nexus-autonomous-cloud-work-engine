## 2. Candidate A — eBRC / exporter remittance reconciliation

### Official process and current substitute boundary
DGFT's 14-Aug-2024 Trade Notice 12/2024-25 introduced bank-to-DGFT IRM API integration and a bulk-upload facility for exporters to self-certify multiple eBRCs using a spreadsheet. The official DGFT exporter guide describes the IRM repository and self-certification flow. This reduces the room for a basic bulk eBRC upload utility.

Official references:
- https://files.capexil.org/2024/09/API-Integration-and-Bulk-Upload-Facility-for-Self-Certification-of-eBRC-Signed.pdf
- https://content.dgft.gov.in/Website/Self_Certification_of_eBRC.pdf

### Direct / adjacent substitutes
1. eBRC.in / Eximfiles — exporter app free at any volume, no certificate cap, no card, bulk filing, automatic IRM-to-shipping-bill/invoice mapping and document extractors; API has a published free general-use allowance of 250 production certificates/month and 10 exporter entities/account. Official pricing: https://ebrc.in/pricing
2. EximJinn — 15-day no-card trial; ₹1,500/month or ₹15,000/year; bulk download/generation, IRM/ORM utilisation, EGM/shipping-bill verification, refund-readiness dashboard. https://www.eximjinn.com/ebrc-reconciliation-software
3. ExportEazy — Free tier; Exporter plan ₹2,999/month + GST or ₹29,990/year + GST; Pro ₹7,999/month + GST or ₹79,990/year + GST; includes payment-to-invoice/shipping-bill matching, eBRC bulk-upload preparation, reminders, Tally export and job margin. 60-day no-card trial is advertised. https://exporteazy.com/
4. OneCompliance / OneTrade by IBDIC — live export-compliance platform for corporates/MSMEs with DGFT integration, IRM processing, eBRC, consignment, banking, document and ERP workflows. https://www.ibdic.in/offerings/onecompliance/
5. DGFT eBRC portal — first-party self-certification and bulk-upload route; not a third-party SaaS but a zero-price substitute for basic filing.
6. ICEGATE — first-party shipping-bill/EGM status and customs workflow; adjacent source of truth, not a replacement for a complete reconciliation workbench.
7. Authorized Dealer bank trade-finance modules — existing remittance/IRM reporting and trade operations; bank-specific, not directly compared on retail SaaS pricing.
8. CargoWise — global freight-forwarding / customs / shipment operations platform; adjacent enterprise substitute.
9. Descartes Global Trade Intelligence / customs solutions — global trade compliance and shipment data; adjacent enterprise substitute.
10. SAP Global Trade Services — enterprise trade compliance and export control; adjacent substitute.
11. Oracle Global Trade Management — enterprise global trade compliance; adjacent substitute.
12. TallyPrime / accounting-led export workflows — accounting and ledger tools with manual or configured export reconciliation; partial substitute.
13. ERPNext / Odoo export workflows — configurable ERP modules/customisation; partial substitute.
14. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central with trade extensions — adjacent ERP and export-document workflow.
15. Bank / CA / export-consultant spreadsheet processes — non-software incumbent substitute; manual and service-heavy, excluded as a product model but relevant to switching friction.

### Disposition
NO-GO for generic eBRC/IRM/shipping-bill reconciliation. A free specialist app/API already covers core exporter filing and matching; paid competitors offer broader export workflow packages at ₹1,500–₹7,999/month. A new entrant would need a distinct, independently evidenced workflow not solved by DGFT, eBRC.in, EximJinn or ExportEazy. No such gap or candidate-specific WTP was established in this batch. This does not close the broader B2B software source family.

## 4. Candidate C — Gumroad as an India digital-product distribution rail

### Current official terms
- India is listed for local bank payouts in INR.
- Standard bank payouts require a $100 balance; India payout day is Tuesday; bank transfers typically take 2–7 business days.
- KYC / valid government ID and proof of residence or business are required; account review can take 1–3 weeks depending on account and sales history.
- Direct/profile sales: 10% + $0.50 per transaction, plus card processing of 2.9% + $0.30 (and any other applicable payment fees).
- Gumroad Discover marketplace sales: 30% per transaction, including processing.
- No monthly platform subscription fee.
- Gumroad's Merchant-of-Record tax handling is not a substitute for reviewing the seller's own Indian income-tax, GST and business obligations.

Official references:
- https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid
- https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees
- https://gumroad.com/pricing

### Disposition
QUALIFIED DISTRIBUTION RAIL; PRODUCT NONE. It can support a zero-monthly-cost digital download or technical publication if an original asset and rights are verified. It does not create demand. Discover's 30% fee materially reduces net contribution; direct sales require an owned or organic discovery path. No listing, KYC, account creation or publication is authorised.
