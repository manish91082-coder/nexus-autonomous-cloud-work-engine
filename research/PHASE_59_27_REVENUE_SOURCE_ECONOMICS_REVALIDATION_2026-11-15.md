## 2. Official evidence revalidated

### 2.1 India software product opportunity — context, not a forecast
RBI's 18-Sep-2026 release for FY2025-26 estimates total Indian software-services exports at US$221.4B, including US$147.0B IT services, US$56.0B BPO, US$12.0B engineering services and US$6.4B software product development. Product development is approximately 2.9% of software-services exports. The aggregate US$221.4B figure must not be presented as the addressable market for a self-serve software vendor or as revenue available to this project.

Official source: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63625

### 2.2 Atlassian Forge marketplace economics — strong rail, hard product gate remains
Official Atlassian Marketplace documentation currently states that, from 1-Oct-2026, paid-via-Atlassian revenue share for Jira/JSM/Confluence apps is 83% for qualifying Forge apps, 75% for Connect and Data Center apps. Qualifying Forge apps receive 100% of gross Forge revenue until $1M lifetime Forge revenue across the partner's Forge apps, then standard share applies. Qualification requires no Connect modules/scopes, OAuth authentication and Forge UI. Remittance starts after $500 profit and is paid within 30 days after the month in which the threshold is accrued; the published window is 30–60 days from sale, with a 30-day refund period.

Interpretation: Atlassian is a credible distribution and monetization rail with meaningful economics, but it requires a narrow buyer problem, compliant Forge implementation, trust/security review and actual paid adoption. The prior research found no concrete product yet passing those gates. Forge remains a high-priority discovery lane, not an automatic product GO.

Official source: https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/marketplace/pricing-payment-and-billing/

### 2.3 Amazon KDP — India payout and royalty conditions
KDP supports Hindi and several Indian-language eBooks. For Amazon.in, the 70% royalty option requires a list price from ₹99 to ₹599; royalty is calculated on list price excluding applicable VAT and delivery costs. KDP pays monthly approximately 60 days after the end of the month in which the sale was reported; India direct deposits can take a further 7–10 business days after payment date.

Interpretation: KDP is a legitimate India-compatible digital publishing rail. But one-off book royalties are not automatically recurring subscription revenue; earnings require original, high-quality IP, discoverability, reviews and sustained sales. No owned catalogue/audience or paid demand evidence is currently recorded. Status: STACKABLE/HOLD, not first source.

Official sources:

### 2.4 Gumroad — India bank payout confirmed, but economics and demand separate
Gumroad's current payout documentation lists India with INR local bank payouts. Standard scheduled payouts require a $100 balance; weekly/monthly/quarterly sales have a minimum 7-day holding period. India bank payouts are scheduled on Tuesday. Account review can take 1–3 weeks depending on account and sales history; KYC is required by its payment processor. The help page distinguishes buyer payment methods (including UPI) from seller payout method.

Interpretation: India payout route is stronger than previously assumed and is confirmed at the public documentation level. It is only a distribution rail. The specific product still needs original IP, demand evidence, fee-adjusted positive unit economics and account-level eligibility. Status: QUALIFIED DISTRIBUTION RAIL; PRODUCT NONE.

Official source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid

### 2.5 YouTube — revenue share exists, but audience threshold is a major dependency
YouTube's official help page states full ad monetization thresholds of 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, followed by channel review. The expanded YPP threshold (where available) starts at 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days and 3,000 watch hours or 3 million Shorts views, but this does not itself confer full ad revenue sharing. Published shares include 55% of net watch-page ad revenue, 45% of allocated Shorts creator-pool revenue and 70% of net fan-funding revenue.

The same official page announces a YPP terms update effective 1-Feb-2027; account owners are expected to review/accept updated terms by 31-Jan-2027.

Interpretation: YouTube can become a distribution/audience asset that supports other products, but it is not an instant autonomous cash engine. It requires original content, audience growth, platform review and continuing policy compliance. No existing channel audience/eligibility evidence is available in this project snapshot. Status: HOLD/STACKABLE, not first source.

Official sources:

- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en-in

### 2.6 Adobe Stock — royalty rates known; catalogue economics unknown
Adobe Stock's contributor documentation, updated 2-Sep-2026, states standard royalty rates of 33% for photos/vectors/illustrations and 35% for video, applied to the net price per licensed asset. This is not a fixed per-download amount; actual royalty depends on licence plan and net price.

Interpretation: scalable catalogue licensing is possible, but requires a sufficiently differentiated original catalogue, acceptance, ongoing discovery and rights clearance. The current project record does not establish an owned, accepted, high-volume asset catalogue or India payout-provider confirmation. Status: HOLD/STACKABLE.

Official source: https://helpx.adobe.com/in/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/royalties-pricing/royalty-rates-assets.html

### 2.7 GitHub Sponsors — low platform fee does not equal demand
GitHub states that personal-account sponsorships have no platform fee (100% of sponsorship amount to the sponsored developer before any other applicable considerations); organization sponsorships can incur up to 6% fees. Becoming a sponsored developer requires eligible open-source contribution, supported region, profile/tier setup, bank/tax information, 2FA and approval.

Interpretation: This is a monetization layer for an already adopted open-source project/community, not a primary product discovery mechanism. No existing project-specific sponsor adoption or inbound sponsorship evidence is recorded. Status: HOLD/STACKABLE.

Official sources:
- https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors-for-open-source-contributors
- https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account

### 2.8 itch.io and Epic Games Store — game economics are attractive only after product/distribution proof
itch.io uses open revenue sharing: the seller chooses the percentage (0–100%) allocated to itch.io. Its documentation describes typical payment processor fees around 2.9% + $0.30, and collected-payout mode has a $5 minimum, tax interview, seven-day transaction wait and staff payout review (typically at least 7 days plus review time). Payout options are PayPal or Payoneer; account-level India compatibility must be confirmed before treating it as a cleared rail.

Epic First Run documentation describes a 100%/0% split on the first $1M net revenue per product per calendar year, followed by 88%/12%, subject to programme conditions.

Interpretation: Platform economics are competitive, but game production, discovery, retention, QA, IP rights and ongoing support remain material. Existing project history contains Orbit Relay NO-GO as-is and no other game with verified demand. Status: HOLD, not first source.

Official sources:
- https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments
- https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-games-store/store-presence/epic-first-run

### 2.9 Apple App Store — commission attractive, but zero-spend conflict
Apple's Small Business Program offers 15% commission to qualifying developers under the $1M proceeds threshold. However, the Apple Developer Program membership is $99/year (or local equivalent). Under the current ₹0-new-spend default, this is not a primary execution rail unless the owner explicitly approves spend or a legitimately available waiver/alternative is verified.

Official sources:
- https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/
- https://developer.apple.com/programs/whats-included/

## 4. Economics discipline

No defensible numeric revenue scenarios are assigned to any product in this batch because there is no verified candidate-specific:
- reachable qualified buyer count;
- price-bearing willingness-to-pay;
- conversion rate;
- retention/renewal rate;
- support burden;
- attributable runtime and payment cost;
- rights-cleared asset inventory;
- owner account eligibility.

The correct model for each recurring SaaS candidate is:
`PCNE = paid active customers × realised net price − platform/payment fees − attributable runtime/delivery − refunds/chargebacks − support/maintenance costs`
with timing and restricted/held balances separately tracked. Any scenario must show customer count, price, conversion, churn, costs and evidence confidence explicitly. No extrapolation from global market size or platform GMV.
