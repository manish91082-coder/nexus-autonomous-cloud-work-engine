6. P59-002 remains BLOCKED; P59-003 remains PAUSED; Phase60 cloud PoC remains deferred. No build, listing, account onboarding, KYC, terms acceptance or spend.

## 4. Greenfield distribution-lane screen — Atlassian Forge audit/compliance evidence

This is a channel/problem screen, not a recommendation to build a compliance product.

### Public marketplace observations

1. **Auditforge** advertises ISO 27001 evidence narratives from Jira/Confluence tickets, citations to source evidence, gap detection and append-only generation/approval logs. It runs on Forge, uses a customer-supplied Anthropic API key, is free for teams up to 10 users and is marked Paid via Atlassian. At the time of the captured Marketplace page it showed **1 install and no reviews**. This is a real competing product and a weak adoption counter, not proof of paid demand.
2. **Audit Trail and Compliance Analytics for Jira** advertises workflow discovery, missing-approval detection, permission drift, control deviations and audit dashboards. The captured Marketplace search result showed **1 install and no reviews**.
3. **Incident Follow-Up Compliance Monitor for JSM** detects resolved incidents without visible post-incident follow-up evidence and is read-only Forge-hosted; its captured Marketplace result showed **no installs yet**.
4. **Request Evidence Control for JSM** already describes review-ready evidence packs with receipts, manifest, source status and checksum metadata, and is paid via Atlassian.
5. **Access Assertions** advertises nightly user-access reviews and exact permission paths for SOC 2/ISO 27001/SOX. The page was discoverable, but this batch did not independently verify its current install or price counters.

### Decision

- Generic Jira audit analytics, AI audit narratives, access review, incident follow-up evidence and evidence-pack export are **NOT SELECTED**. Existing product overlap is direct; the observed low install counters do not prove a gap, nor do they establish that buyers will pay.
- A genuinely different Jira/JSM buyer problem may remain researchable, but must first produce two independent demand signals from buyers, procurement or documented recurring operational pain, plus a 10–15 substitute audit and price-bearing WTP. Marketplace listing presence alone is insufficient.
- Atlassian's 83% Forge share from 1-Oct-2026 and qualifying 100% Forge share up to $1M lifetime revenue improve platform economics but cannot rescue a weak product. Paid remittance still begins after $500 profit and is remitted 30–60 days after sales accrual under the published terms.
- Do not register a partner account, accept terms, publish or request owner credentials as part of research.
