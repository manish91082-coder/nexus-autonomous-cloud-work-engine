## 3. Tender channel decision
- NeGD: closed; remove from live pipeline.
- C-DAC: closed and incompatible; remove from pipeline.

- Do not invent a consortium/subcontracting path. It must be expressly allowed by the RFE and validated with the prime's written work allocation and contract terms.
- Avoid EMD/fees/registration/consultant charges without explicit approval.

## 4. Strategic correction
The institutional tender route is currently a slow, high-barrier channel, not the near-term cash engine. Keep it as a selective pipeline. Prioritize faster paid contract routes and partner delivery where eligibility and payment protections are documented. Continue direct B2B recurring buyer validation in parallel. Do not let tender research monopolize project execution.
