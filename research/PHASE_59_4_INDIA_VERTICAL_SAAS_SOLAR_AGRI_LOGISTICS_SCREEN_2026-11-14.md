## 2. Candidate A — Solar EPC / rooftop installer operations

### Buyer job and workflow
Indian rooftop EPCs coordinate leads, site surveys, design/BOM, quotations, consumer documents, DISCOM feasibility/net-metering, installation, inspection, commissioning, subsidy paperwork and post-installation service/AMC. Government PM-Surya Ghar operational guidelines envisage National Portal and State DISCOM portal integration, including net-metering, load sanctions and inspections. IEEFA's 2025 analysis reports persistent portal glitches, document upload errors and subsidy-related delays.

### Demand evidence
- PM-Surya Ghar official operational guidelines establish a multi-stage portal/approval workflow: https://www.meecl.nic.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Annexure-C-Operational-Guidelines-for-the-Implementation-of-PM-Surya-Ghar-Muft-Bijli-Yojana.pdf

### Disposition

## 3. Candidate B — Agri-input distribution / dealer operations

### Buyer job and workflow
Fertiliser, seed and pesticide distributors/dealers must manage batch/expiry, licensed supplier records, seasonal demand, dealer/farmer credit, collections, returns, product traceability, field orders, dispatch and regulated PoS records. A high-value recurring job could be preserving a single auditable chain from batch receipt to sale/return/claim/collection across distributor, warehouse and dealer.

### Government field evidence (historical baseline)
The Government of India's *Agricultural Situation in India*, July 2022, includes an agro-economic study of fertilizer DBT at retail point based on a sample across 13 states: 780 retailers, 1,300 top buyers, 650 frequent buyers and 1,300 random-walk buyers. It reported:
- 57% of surveyed retailers relied on manual books for stock records; 12% used Tally/computer records.
- Only 58% agreed PoS stock matched physical stock; 42% disagreed.
- Network/software/biometric and delayed company stock-invoice issues were reported.
- This is 2022 evidence, not a 2026 adoption estimate. It establishes historical operational failure modes that need fresh validation, not a current market-size claim.
Official source: https://desagri.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Agricultural-Situation-in-India-July-2022.pdf (pp. 33–36; study results).

### Current problem and market signals
A current India agri-input store guide describes batch/expiry, licence registers, farmer credit and PoS reconciliation as core operating needs; it notes generic GST billing often lacks mandatory batch/expiry and seasonal-credit features. This is a trade guide, not an independent survey: https://www.thesaral.com/start-a-business/agri-input-store/software
A current supplier-vetting guide documents seasonal supplier credit, return windows, near-expiry stock and batch traceability concerns; vendor terms vary and require local verification: https://www.thesaral.com/start-a-business/agri-input-store/finding-suppliers

### Substitute density and price signals
At least nine directly relevant products were found:
- OP Software pesticides/seeds/fertilizers billing: ₹3,000/year after first free month; licence printing, batch/expiry, farmer credit and Hindi/Punjabi bills.
- AgroKhata: from ₹2,999/month or ₹8,499/3 months; billing, farmer credit, stock/expiry and CA exports.
- AgriPeople CRM: plans from ₹2,999/month; dealer credit, orders, collections, WhatsApp and Tally sync.
- Pinakexa: agri-input ERP with dealer/distributor orders, seasonal planning, batch/expiry, credit and collections; demo-led.
- TradeX (Quantbit): agricultural-input dealer/distributor ERP with batch/expiry, seasonal replenishment, credit, dispatch and margin.
- Tathven: agri-input ERP with batch/expiry, traceability, godowns, GST, credit, WhatsApp CRM and manufacturing.
- xMatix: field sales, seasonal schemes, credit/liquidation, dealer self-service and inventory.
- ZunderFlow: dealer ordering, batch/lot/expiry, field orders, offline mobile workflow, dispatch proof and payment links.
- AgroLynk (Orivil): cloud ERP for agro dealers with inventory, GST billing, farmer accounts and multi-branch operations.

### Disposition
**NEXT RESEARCH PRIORITY, NOT PRODUCT GO.** Compared with generic solar EPC software, this vertical has stronger historical government field evidence of inventory/PoS record mismatch and recurring seasonal workflows. However, substitute density is already high and some dealer products are priced very cheaply (e.g., ₹3,000/year). A generic dealer ERP would be hard to defend. Research must narrow to a distributor-level, multi-branch or batch-to-claim reconciliation workflow where financial loss and buyer budget are independently evidenced. Verify current 2026 workflow and state-specific legal/PoS requirements; do not build any substitute for official PoS or bypass its controls.

## 4. Candidate C — Small-fleet / SME logistics operations

### Buyer job and evidence
A 2026 technology-enabled intra-city logistics study estimates a fragmented supply base of about 12.5 million trucks and 3.5 million operators, many with five or fewer vehicles, and describes uncertain availability, ETA, manual call/message bookings and freight-expense reconciliation. Deloitte's road-logistics report also documents fragmentation and low digital capability, but is older.
Sources:
https://zup-promotional-image.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/c-dep.org/assets/STUDY_OF_TECHNOLOGY_ENABLED_INTRA_CITY_LOGISTICS_SERVICES_f149ccb282.pdf
https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/in/Documents/consumer-business/Time%20of%20Reckoning_print.pdf

### Substitute density
Large and mid-market alternatives include Locus, Shipsy, FarEye, LogiNext, ClickPost, TrackoMile and Tookan. Many enterprise platforms use custom quotes; Tookan has a published self-serve starting plan around US$99/month in one current comparison. The broad TMS/last-mile category is not empty.
Source: https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/best-delivery-management-software-india

### Disposition
**HOLD.** Structural fragmentation is attractive, but small-fleet buyers may have low software budgets, fragmented onboarding and high support/integration needs. This conflicts with self-serve, zero-spend and low-manual-fulfilment constraints unless a very narrow transaction workflow is identified.
