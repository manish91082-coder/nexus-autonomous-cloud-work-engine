## 2. Evidence by source family

### A. Self-serve digital products and technical publishing — strongest immediate distribution/payout feasibility, but not yet a product GO
- Gumroad's official payout guide explicitly lists India local-bank payouts in INR; buyer checkout may support UPI, but checkout method does not determine seller payout. Payout timing/holding rules and fees still apply. Source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid
- Etsy India currently allows new shops only for international sales; requires GSTIN and Payoneer, charges a one-time US$10 setup fee, and Indian digital listings cannot be purchased by buyers in India. This fails the strict zero-spend default and limits domestic demand. Source: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/6742925359255-How-to-Accept-Payments-as-a-Seller-in-India
- Amazon KDP supports Hindi and other Indian-language eBooks, but paperback publishing in Indian languages is not available. India Kindle 70% royalty price band is ₹99–₹599 under eligibility conditions; 35% option has a wider price range. Royalties are not equivalent to net PCNE after production, refunds, price matching and other deductions. Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202124400 ; https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560 ; https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210
- Lemon Squeezy payout documentation says India is not in the ordinary bank-payout country list and references Stripe India invite-only onboarding; without pre-approved Stripe, seller payout relies on PayPal. Therefore India seller payout is conditional, not cleared. Sources: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries ; https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/getting-paid
- These are viable distribution mechanics, not proof that a new catalogue will be discovered or purchased. No independent buyer demand, product-specific WTP, conversion rate or repeat purchase was established in this batch.

### B. Stock assets — scalable catalogue model, weak revenue predictability
- Adobe Stock states standard royalties of 33% for photos/vectors/illustrations and 35% for video, calculated on the net attributable licence price. Payout requires at least US$25, 45 days after first sale, and an eligible PayPal/Payoneer/Skrill account. Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/in/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/royalties-pricing/royalty-rates-assets.html ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/payment-taxes/payment-requirements.html
- Shutterstock says contributors can earn up to 40% depending on level; this is not a guaranteed base rate. Source: https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594653-contributor-earnings-structure-faq
- The model can compound with catalogue size but needs high-volume differentiated assets, moderation acceptance and marketplace search visibility. No current owned catalogue, asset acceptance rate, download history or buyer demand data is evidenced here. Not selected as first engine.

### C. Software/IP licensing and white-label/OEM
- Perpetual, reseller, white-label, OEM and usage-based licences are legitimate models, but public generic model descriptions do not establish buyer demand for this owner's software or prove rights to source assets. A licensing route needs verified ownership, complete deployable code, documentation, support boundary, licence terms and buyer-side evidence.
- Tech Cloud ERP remains a user-reported 35-module asset family; no source tree, ownership documents, module inventory or deployment evidence has been inspected in the canonical repository. Do not offer or license it until that audit is completed.
- Existing canonical products were audited in Phase 59.2; none passed first-product gate. AgentMargin is NO-GO as a generic one-time calculator (Phase 59.3). No new licensing candidate qualifies.

### D. API / usage-based monetization
- RapidAPI documentation states a flat 20% marketplace fee on API Hub payments; a $10 plan yields $8 before any provider-side payout costs or API operating costs. Source: https://docs.rapidapi.com/v2/docs/payouts-and-finance
- This establishes marketplace fee mechanics only. It does not establish India provider payout eligibility, category-specific buyer demand, pricing power, source/data rights, runtime margin or API reliability. API products should only be advanced where the owner controls a scarce, lawful function/data source and usage has repeat value.
- No such qualified API asset was identified in this batch.

### E. Open-source sponsorship
- GitHub Sponsors lists India among supported regions; personal payouts require a supported-region bank account and tax/profile setup. Sources: https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account ; https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud%40latest/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors
- GitHub reports aggregate ecosystem totals, but aggregate sponsorship totals do not predict this project’s sponsorship. Sponsorship depends on an adopted useful project, maintainer trust and recurring community value. No project-specific sponsor demand or sponsor commitment exists. Treat as a later stackable source, not first engine.

## 4. Decision and next sequence
1. Do not build a product from this batch. No product-specific WTP, buyer count, conversion or positive fully-loaded unit economics has been established.
2. The strongest *operational rail candidate* to examine next is a Gumroad-distributed, self-serve digital product because India INR bank payout is explicitly listed and zero-upfront listing is not contradicted by the evidence reviewed. This is only a distribution hypothesis, not product selection.
3. Next research must identify a narrowly useful, original technical asset from existing work (not duplicate/free generic templates), audit 10–15 substitutes, prove two independent demand signals and price-bearing WTP, check platform fees/refunds/support and model conservative net contribution.
4. In parallel, prepare the Tech Cloud ERP evidence checklist, but do not claim ownership or commercial rights until source and ownership are inspected.
5. Keep KDP, stock assets, OEM licensing, API and Sponsors in the portfolio map as potential stackable sources. Do not count the same product across multiple storefronts as multiple engines.
6. P59-002 remains BLOCKED. No account creation, KYC, terms acceptance, listing, public release or spend without the relevant owner gate.

## 5. Confidence and limitations
- High confidence in the quoted platform terms as surfaced in the official documentation at research time.
- Low confidence in obtainable revenue: no independent customer interviews, paid orders, conversion data, creator acceptance statistics or repeat-purchase cohort evidence were obtained.
- No TAM-to-revenue extrapolation is made. Platform payout support is not seller-account approval or guaranteed payout.
