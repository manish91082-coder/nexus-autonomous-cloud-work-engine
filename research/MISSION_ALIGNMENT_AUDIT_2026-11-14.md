## 1. Single final goal — unchanged

Build a portfolio of lawful, self-serve, Internet-native software and digital products that can be configured once and then produce repeatable, scalable revenue with maximum practical automation.

**Primary financial target: ₹10,00,00,000 of verified Platform-Credited Net Earnings (PCNE)** in the owner's eligible seller/creator/provider balances on approved revenue platforms.

The target is cumulative verified net earnings, not:
- gross customer sales or GMV;
- projected ARR/MRR, valuation or market size;
- app installs, downloads, listing count or GitHub stars;
- unearned platform credits, grants, test transactions or self-purchases;
- money that is pending, reversible, disputed, held or forfeitable.

PCNE requires a real independent buyer transaction, an authoritative platform earning credit, and deduction/reservation of marketplace commissions, processing fees, direct usage/compute/storage costs, refunds, reversals and chargebacks. Bank settlement is tracked separately and must not be double-counted when the platform balance is withdrawn.

## 2. Business model and hard rules

**Preferred:** recurring SaaS, marketplace apps, AI applications/agents, developer/business utilities, APIs, automation products and original digital software/IP with demonstrated buyer demand.

**Delivery:** self-serve onboarding, digital fulfilment, repeatable updates and maximum practical automation.

**Default capital:** ₹0 new spend. Prefer existing assets, free tiers and open-source infrastructure. Paid registrations, accounts, APIs, hosting, domains, advertising, subscriptions and chargeable tests require explicit owner approval.

**Excluded as the core engine:** employment, job offers, freelance bidding, manual gigs, recurring human implementation, bespoke consulting fulfilment, fabricated buyers, fake reviews, spam, unauthorized scraping, policy bypass and data resale without rights.
