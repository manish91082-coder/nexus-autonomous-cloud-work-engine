## 2. Candidate and current truth

**Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk v0.3.0**
- Offline-first, single HTML file; no server, external scripts or network calls.
- Tracks placement fees, installment receipts, outstanding balances, commissions earned only on collected cash, guarantee claims/events, invoice due dates, receivable ageing, payment-linked guarantee flags, client concentration and JSON backups.
- No candidate PII.
- Browser QA and deterministic formula/security tests were previously recorded as passing.
- It is not an ATS, CRM, cloud accounting system, invoice issuer, messaging system or legal interpreter.
- Product demand, willingness to pay and independent sales remain unproven.
- Previous prices ₹499/₹799/₹999 were hypotheses only.

## 3. What Phase 50 must prove

### Buyer and job-to-be-done
Target: small recruitment agencies already using an ATS, Tally/Zoho or spreadsheets. Determine whether they have a recurring, material gap around:
- partial placement-fee receipts;
- commission payable only on actual collection;
- contractual invoice due-date ageing;
- guarantee/replacement event history;
- privacy-preserving financial control without candidate data.

The product is not qualified merely because recruitment agencies exist or because late payments are common. The buyer must already experience this exact workflow problem and have an unmet need after considering current alternatives.

### Ground-level competitor recheck
Revalidate current products and price/feature claims for MoneyGence, IT Ways, WePlacement, HireXL and any new direct competitors. Compare:
- actual cash/instalment receipt handling;
- commission calculation and payment ledger;
- invoice due-date ageing;
- guarantee clause/event handling;
- offline use / no candidate PII;
- user counts, pricing, free tiers, marketplace presence and recent updates where visible.

A product with more features is not automatically better. The proposed distinction must be simple enough to explain in one sentence and hard for free alternatives to erase.

### Getvik distribution evidence
Use current official pages and visible category/product activity to answer:
- Does Getvik's Business & Finance category contain relevant agency/HR/finance buyers or mainly low-price Excel templates?
- Are category-displayed sales counters current, and what exactly do they count?
- Does the platform create discoverability, or must the creator bring all traffic?
- What are the exact Free-plan seller fees, gateway charges, payout conditions, refunds, licence controls and seller support obligations today?

Marketplace counters are seller/platform displays, not independently audited revenue. Category sales are not demand for this particular product.

### Product economics and revenue scale
Model transparent cases only:
- one-time gross sale price;
- Getvik platform fee and GST on fee;
- processor charge and GST;
- refunds/reversals and attributable costs;
- creator's platform-credited net earnings;
- number of paid orders needed for ₹1 lakh, ₹10 lakh, ₹1 crore and ₹10 crore.

Do not claim these order counts are achievable. Show the traffic/conversion requirements as mathematical sensitivities, with no invented conversion rate. If the path to ₹10 Cr requires a practically implausible number of low-value downloads, do not present it as the main scalable engine.

### Self-serve and automation constraint
The primary acquisition and delivery path must not require:
- cold outreach;
- live sales calls or demos;
- manual consulting/implementation;
- repeated personal follow-ups;
- recurring bespoke setup.

The digital delivery can be automated by the marketplace, but discoverability and customer support duties still need evidence. A one-time owner approval/KYC is allowed. Mandatory platform terms, seller identity, licence, refund and support obligations cannot be bypassed.

## 4. GO / NO-GO criteria

GO only if all are supported:
1. The exact agency finance workflow gap remains inadequately served by direct competitors/free tools.
2. Getvik reaches the intended buyer segment with credible marketplace discovery or another already-approved product-led channel.
3. Current official fees and seller obligations produce positive platform-credited net contribution.
4. A one-time download price can support a plausible path to meaningful scale, not just a token first sale.
5. Offline/no-candidate-PII has measurable buyer value rather than being an engineer's preference.
6. Product QA, documentation, license, privacy, refund wording and support contact are complete.
7. Owner-only KYC, seller terms and public publication can be handled through the real account without spending money.

If any material gate fails: NO-GO or reposition; keep the product private.

## 5. Revenue scale arithmetic — not a forecast

For a one-time purchase:
- At ₹499 gross, 2,005,000 orders are needed for about ₹10 Cr gross sales.
- At ₹799 gross, 1,251,565 orders are needed.
- At ₹999 gross, 1,001,002 orders are needed.

These are gross-sales arithmetic, not net earnings. Actual order counts required for ₹10 Cr PCNE will be higher after marketplace and payment fees, refunds and other costs. No conversion rate, traffic level or timeline is assumed.

This makes the key question: **Can this product achieve a genuinely large, repeatable self-serve distribution engine?** If not, it can at most be an early revenue experiment, not the core ₹10 Cr path.
