## 4. MSME cash conversion and TReDS
PIB reports a 30 June 2026 notification requiring operating CPSEs to route settlement of goods/services invoices procured from MSMEs through RBI-authorised TReDS platforms. PIB states MSME suppliers may discount approved invoices; five operating platforms listed: RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart, C2treds and DTX. FY 2025-26 invoice discounting reported at ₹3.47 lakh crore.
Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283195&lang=1&reg=20
This is a receivable-finance option, not a guarantee of immediate payment or free finance. Eligibility, buyer acceptance, invoice approval, financier bids, discount rate, recourse, charges and registration must be checked. The project must not assume Udyam/MSME status or CPSE eligibility until documentary proof is verified.
Decision: add TReDS-aware cashflow planning to future CPSE/MSME contracts. Never price a contract assuming discounted invoice cash is guaranteed.

## 5. Strategic interpretation
The government channel has two possible routes:
A. Prime contractor: requires tender-specific eligibility, financial capacity, credentials, EMD/security and delivery capability. Not assumed available.
B. Subcontractor/technology partner: partner with an already eligible empanelled prime; only if the tender/work order permits subcontracting and a written agreement protects scope, acceptance, payment, IP, data security and liability. This is the more realistic route to investigate, not yet validated.

## 6. Anti-drift / no-go controls
- Do not spend weeks preparing bids with no eligibility evidence.
- Do not pay EMD, tender fees, registration or partner fees without explicit approval.
- Do not claim government credentials, turnover, past performance or certifications not evidenced.
- Do not treat empanelment, RFE, EOI, shortlist or award notice as revenue.
- Do not sign or accept terms, submit a bid, contact a buyer or partner without explicit approval.
- Marketplace remains only one acquisition channel; institutional procurement is a separate channel.

6. Create a cash conversion checklist for CPSE/MSME contracts, including TReDS only where eligible and invoice is accepted.
