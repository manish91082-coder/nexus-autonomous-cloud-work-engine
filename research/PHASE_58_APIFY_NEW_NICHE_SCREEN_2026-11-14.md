## 1. Scope and exclusions
Phase 49 already rejected 27 generic raw-data/extraction Actors across web/RAG, PDF/OCR, ClinicalTrials.gov, SEC/EDGAR, Federal Register, USAspending/SAM.gov, domain/RDAP and ATS job postings. This screen avoids cloning those categories and screens five additional generic families:
1. E-commerce product/price monitoring.
2. App Store / Google Play review monitoring.
3. Website and third-party service-status monitoring.
4. Vendor changelog / documentation / API-change monitoring.
5. Product recall feeds and merchant-catalog exposure matching.

This is a bounded first screen, not a census of Apify Store use cases. Listing presence and published price are substitute signals, not evidence of paid demand for a new product.
