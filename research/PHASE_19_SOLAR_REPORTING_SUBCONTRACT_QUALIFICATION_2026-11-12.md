# Phase 19 — Solar O&M Reporting Subcontract Qualification
**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Repository:** `manish91082-coder/nexus-autonomous-cloud-work-engine`  
**Decision status:** Research completed; no qualified funded reporting-only assignment found.

## 1. Executive decision
The market has a demonstrable recurring reporting and data-reconciliation workload. However, public evidence reviewed in this phase does not establish a currently open, funded, independently subcontractable reporting-only assignment for this project. The strongest direct task-level signal was a monthly renewable-asset report marketplace listing, but it is closed and crowded. Several firms explicitly sell O&M reporting or SCADA analytics, but that makes them competitors, prime contractors, or potential channels—not proven buyers of our subcontracted service.

**Phase 19 outcome:** no bid, outreach, registration, KYC, customer-data access, paid API, or product build. Continue only with buyer-side qualification.

## 2. Evidence classification
Evidence classes:
- **A — Direct buyer request:** buyer explicitly asks for the service and provides a live request.
- **B — Explicit subcontract/partner mechanism:** public page invites a relevant delivery partner or subcontractor, but no specific paid work order is evidenced.
- **C — Service capability / market demand:** vendor offers the service; this does not prove it buys external delivery.
- **D — Procurement requirement:** reporting is embedded in a larger contract; not independently addressable unless subcontracting is permitted.
- **X — Closed/stale/unsuitable:** not actionable.

URL: https://www.freelancer.com/projects/data-analysis/Monthly-Renewable-Asset-Report

- Listing described a recurring monthly report for a portfolio of solar, wind and hybrid plants across nine Indian states plus international rooftop sites.

- Displayed budget ₹12,500–₹37,500; paid on delivery; 45 proposals; listing status **Closed** when inspected.
- **Class:** X (historical direct buyer signal, now closed).
- **Value:** unusually close match to the reporting-only service hypothesis and validates the deliverable shape, not a live sales opportunity.
- **Blockers:** closed; 45 proposals; paid-on-delivery with no escrow proof; no verified solar analytics portfolio artifact in this project; listing alone does not validate buyer payment reliability.
- **Decision:** no application and no claim of prior solar portfolio experience.

## 4. Market interpretation
The evidence supports three distinct markets:
1. **Monitoring software:** SCADA, RMS, fleet dashboards and automated report generation.
2. **Technical asset management / owner's engineering:** reconcile meter and SCADA data, review O&M vendors, validate performance and report to asset owners.
3. **Field O&M / EPC:** maintenance, site personnel, cleaning, repairs, safety, response SLAs and generation guarantees.

Our proposed service sits narrowly between data operations and owner-side reporting QA. It must not compete by claiming to diagnose faults, guarantee PR/availability, operate SCADA or perform field O&M.

The strongest potential buying logic is not "we can make a dashboard." It is: a contractor/asset manager has a recurring monthly close deadline, raw data from multiple OEMs, manual reconciliation burden, exceptions that delay owner reports or invoices, and a permitted budget for an independently reviewed reporting work package.

## 5. Narrow service package for future validation
**Solar O&M Reporting QA and Monthly Evidence Pack**

Input (buyer-authorized, preferably redacted sample first):
- CSV/XLSX exports from SCADA/inverter portals;
- site/plant and meter mapping supplied by buyer;
- target/baseline generation values and reporting calendar supplied by buyer;
- fault/downtime/curtailment records supplied and classified by authorized O&M personnel;
- prior approved report template.

Outputs:
- source-file inventory and ingestion log;
- schema/unit/timestamp validation;
- duplicate, missing interval and inconsistent-total exception register;
- site-wise and portfolio-wise actual-versus-buyer-supplied-target tables;
- arithmetic reconciliation to source totals, with unresolved differences explicitly flagged;
- daily/monthly generation and downtime summaries using buyer-provided classifications;
- draft charts and monthly report pack;
- source-to-output traceability and reviewer sign-off checklist.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Every reported numeric total links to source file, sheet/table and row/interval range.
2. Reconciliation differences are either zero or explicitly listed with magnitude and unresolved status.
3. No missing interval is silently interpolated or represented as measured generation.
4. All units, time zones, meter boundaries and target definitions are declared.
5. Fault cause, curtailment cause, PR/availability formula and guarantee status are never inferred without buyer-approved definitions.
6. Final output is marked draft until buyer's qualified reviewer approves it.

Exclusions:
- no SCADA control or plant command;
- no electrical diagnosis, root-cause determination or safety sign-off;
- no plant maintenance/cleaning/repair;
- no PR/availability/generation guarantees;
- no regulatory certification or independent engineering opinion;
- no data access absent written owner/prime authorization;
- no customer communications or invoicing without explicit approval.

## 6. Commercial qualification gate
A prospect becomes actionable only after all are confirmed:
- named legal buyer and authorized signatory;
- specific active portfolio and reporting period;
- buyer confirms it may subcontract this exact data/reporting work;
- written scope and sample report template;
- authorized sample data and data handling/security requirements;
- cadence, number of plants, data volume and exception definitions;
- named reviewer and objective acceptance criteria;
- fixed fee or rate, payment currency, tax/withholding and transfer fees;
- advance/milestone/weekly payment or platform escrow, with first milestone funded before work;
- liability boundary and prime contract flow-down terms reviewed;
- positive expected net receipts after all actual costs.

No work begins on a promise of future volume, an unfunded purchase order, or a pay-when-paid clause without protection.

**Next phase recommendation:** move beyond generic solar O&M directories. Search for (a) owner-side asset managers with multi-OEM portfolios and manual monthly reporting, (b) O&M contractors with published tender awards/portfolio evidence but lean back-office capacity, and (c) explicit analytics/reporting subcontract scopes in tender addenda or partner calls. A candidate must have a live operational trigger and a legally separable scope before any outreach is proposed.
