# Phase 36 — 29-candidate revenue opportunity scorecard (2026-11-12)

## Method
The published rubric weights demand (25), India payout certainty/speed (20), automation (20), net economics/zero-upfront feasibility (15), distribution/differentiation (10), and legal/IP/security/platform risk (10).

- 80–100: pilot priority
- 70–79: bounded validation pilot
- 55–69: validation only / hold for evidence
- below 55: reject or archive unless a specific hard-gate change occurs
- Hard gates override numerical scores.

Scores are preliminary decision aids based on official/platform-published terms, public marketplace signals and documented unknowns. They are not independent market-size estimates, sales forecasts or guarantees.

## Portfolio conclusion
- 29 candidates across 17 categories are now scored or explicitly hard-gate rejected.
- 14 payout routes/providers are documented in `control/payout_registry.json`; country/account-level unknowns remain explicit.
- No candidate scores 80+, so no platform is treated as a fully validated pilot.
- Getvik (78) is the best bounded validation candidate; ownstreet (74) is secondary after one genuine paid sale; Topmate (72) and Payhip+Razorpay (70) are alternates.
- Getvik's public catalogue shows platform-reported completed sales for Excel and educational downloads. This is category-level demand signal only; it does not prove demand for our recruitment finance software.
- No paid seller plan is justified. No platform account or KYC is activated.

## Product linkage
Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk v0.3.0 has invoice due dates, late-payment detection and user-configured payment-linked guarantee flags. It passed formula/mission CI, Chromium/Firefox browser QA and private packaging. It remains private pending product-specific demand and owner approval.
