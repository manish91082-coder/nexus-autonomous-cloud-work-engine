# Private Listing Draft — Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk

**Status:** Private draft only. Not published. Owner approval required.

## Title
Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk — Installment Collections, Recruiter Commission & Guarantee Control

## Short description
An offline-first revenue-control desk for recruitment agencies. Reconcile placement fee installments, actual cash collected, recruiter commission payable versus paid, guarantee claims and replacement events, ageing and client concentration. Uses anonymous business references and stores no candidate personal data.

## Includes
- Placement fee and installment-level cash receipt ledger
- Outstanding fee and collection efficiency
- Recruiter commission earned only on collected cash, with paid/unpaid reconciliation
- Guarantee claim, replacement and resolution event history
- 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ receivable ageing
- Client-wise outstanding concentration
- Guarantee expiry watchlist
- JSON backup/restore and legacy v0.1.0 migration
- Hindi user guide

## Who it is for
Small recruitment agencies, boutique staffing firms and recruitment business owners who already have an ATS or separate candidate system but need tighter cash conversion and post-placement financial control.

## Not included
Candidate sourcing, CV database, job posting, ATS, email/WhatsApp messaging, payroll, GST/TDS computation, invoicing, legal contract interpretation or consulting.

## Calculation and limitations
Commission is calculated only on actual cash receipts. Ageing currently uses placement date as a proxy because invoice due dates are not yet captured; it is an operational signal, not statutory accounting ageing. No product-market fit or willingness-to-pay evidence has been established.
