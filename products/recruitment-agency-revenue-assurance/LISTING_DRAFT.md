# Private Listing Draft — Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk

**Status:** Not published. Owner approval required.

## Title
Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk — Placement Fee, Collections & Guarantee Tracker

## Short description
A privacy-first offline control desk for recruitment agency owners. Track placement fees booked, cash collected, outstanding receivables, recruiter share and replacement-guarantee exposure without storing candidate personal data or paying for an ATS subscription.

## Includes
- Placement fee and collection ledger
- Outstanding fee calculation
- Recruiter share on collected cash
- Guarantee end-date and 14-day expiry watchlist
- Collection ratio dashboard
- Local JSON backup and restore
- Hindi user guide

## Who it is for
Small recruitment agencies, boutique staffing firms and independent recruitment business owners who need visibility into placement revenue and post-placement obligations.

## Not included
Candidate sourcing, CV database, email/WhatsApp messaging, job posting, ATS, payroll, GST/TDS computation, invoicing, legal contract interpretation or consulting.

## Pricing hypothesis
Test ₹499, ₹999 and ₹1,499 only after product QA and buyer interviews/interest signals. Existing competitors range from low-cost templates to higher-priced dashboards; no willingness-to-pay has been established for this product. These are experiments, not revenue forecasts.

## Owner gate
Do not publish, accept platform terms or connect payout accounts without explicit owner approval.
