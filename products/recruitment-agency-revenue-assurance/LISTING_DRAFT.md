# Private Getvik Listing Draft — Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk v0.3.0

**Status:** Private draft only. Not published. Getvik account/KYC/legal acceptance not completed. Owner approval required.

## Product title
Recruitment Agency Cashflow & Guarantee Control Desk — Offline Software v0.3.0

## Short description
An offline-first business utility for recruitment agency owners to reconcile placement-fee installments, actual cash collections, recruiter commission earned versus paid, guarantee claims/replacements, receivable ageing and client concentration. No candidate personal data is required or stored.

## What is included
- Offline HTML application for modern desktop browsers
- Placement/client/recruiter anonymous reference ledger
- Installment-level actual cash receipt log
- Outstanding placement fee calculation
- Recruiter commission earned only on cash actually collected
- Recruiter commission paid/unpaid reconciliation
- Guarantee claim, replacement and resolution event log
- Invoice reference, invoice date and contractual due date
- Due-date-based receivable ageing, with placement-date proxy visibly marked if due date is missing
- Late-payment detection and user-configured payment-linked guarantee risk flags (operational reminders only, not legal interpretation)
- Client concentration views
- Guarantee expiry watchlist
- JSON backup and restore
- Hindi user guide

## Who it is for
Small recruitment agencies and boutique staffing businesses that already manage candidates in another system and need a separate cash-realization and guarantee-obligation control tool.

## Not included
ATS, job board, candidate database, CV storage, sourcing, job placement service, manual recruitment, email/WhatsApp outreach, invoicing, accounting, GST/TDS calculation, legal contract interpretation, cloud sync, multi-user collaboration, automatic updates or bespoke consulting.

## Compatibility and data
- Single HTML file, no external scripts, server, analytics or network calls.
- Works locally in a modern browser.
- Data is stored in local browser storage on the device. Users must export and secure JSON backups.
- Do not enter candidate names, phone numbers, email addresses, CVs, Aadhaar, PAN or other personal/sensitive data.
- Receivable ageing uses the contractual invoice due date when supplied; if missing, placement date is visibly marked as a proxy. This is not statutory accounting ageing.
