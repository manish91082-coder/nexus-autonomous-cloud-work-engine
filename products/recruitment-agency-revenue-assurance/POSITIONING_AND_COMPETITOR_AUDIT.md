# Recruitment Agency Revenue Assurance Desk — Positioning & Competitor Audit

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Product:** v0.3.0 private draft  
**Decision:** PRIVATE_VALIDATION_HOLD; no further feature build or publication until demand evidence.

## 2. Evidence-based implications
- “Recruitment CRM with invoices and commissions” is not differentiated. Competitors already advertise these capabilities.
- MoneyGence is the strongest direct threat to a generic finance dashboard because it explicitly targets recruitment agencies and offers a free first-two-client tier.
- IT Ways and WePlacement already cover payment ageing, recruiter splits and guarantee countdowns; adding similar features would be feature-chasing.
- Our product's defensible hypothesis is narrower: offline-first, no-account, no-candidate-PII financial-control desk that complements an existing ATS, with installment-level cash reconciliation and commission earned only from actual collected receipts, plus explicit guarantee event history.
- That hypothesis is not yet proven to be valuable enough to displace free products or spreadsheets. Offline-only may also be a limitation for multi-user teams.

## 3. Narrow target hypothesis
Small/solo recruitment agencies that already use a separate ATS or spreadsheet for candidates, but need a private owner-only finance layer without importing candidate records or sharing financial data with a cloud ATS. They need to answer: what cash actually arrived, what commission is earned from collected cash, which invoices are overdue, and what guarantee exposure remains.

## 4. What we must NOT claim
- Do not claim we are the only recruitment finance product.
- Do not claim statutory GST compliance or replace accounting software/CA workflows.
- Do not claim guaranteed collections, legal guarantee validity or automated payment recovery.
- Do not claim customer demand, retention or willingness to pay.
- Do not compare against competitor marketing claims as independently audited performance.

## 5. Go / hold / stop decision
**HOLD paid launch and further feature development.** First run a no-publication validation with 5–8 small agency owners. Show the existing private build and ask them to complete real tasks using synthetic data. Record whether they already solve these workflows with Excel, Tally, MoneyGence, IT Ways, WePlacement or another ATS; what breaks; and whether offline/no-candidate-PII is a real buying reason.

Directional kill criteria:
- Stop if 4/5 or more target users say their current free/low-cost solution already handles these exact finance workflows adequately.
- Reposition only if at least 3/5 independently identify a costly gap our current build solves and complete the core tasks without coaching.
- Do not price or launch based on compliments. A paid pilot requires explicit buyer commitment or actual purchase evidence.
