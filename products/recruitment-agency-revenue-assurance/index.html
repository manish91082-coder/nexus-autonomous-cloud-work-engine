Policy and calculation notes

  • Outstanding = booked placement fee minus cash collected.
  • Recruiter share due = collected amount × configured share percentage.
  • Guarantee end = placement date + configured guarantee days.
  • Receivable ageing uses invoice due date; if absent, placement date is a visible proxy.
  • Payment-linked guarantee flags are operational reminders based on the user-selected contract clause, not legal conclusions.
  • Only cash actually collected should be entered in collected amount.
  • Tax, GST, TDS, legal contract interpretation and invoice compliance are outside this tool.
  • No candidate PII should be stored. Use a separate compliant ATS for candidate records.
