How to use

Inputs should come from actual stock movement and supplier history.

  • Use a representative sales/usage period.
  • Update lead time when supplier performance changes.
  • Safety stock is a management choice, not a universal constant.
  • Review slow-moving, expired or obsolete inventory separately.
