**Status:** Private draft only. Do not publish until owner approval and platform payout/KYC checks.

## Product title
MSME Ops Desk Lite — Offline Business Calculator & Daily Operations Toolkit

## Short description
A lightweight, offline-first toolkit for Indian micro and small businesses. Calculate margins and break-even, plan stock reorder points, track receivables and record daily sales — without an account, subscription or cloud upload.

## Product description
Get a practical operations desk without adopting a full ERP.

MSME Ops Desk Lite runs as a single HTML file in your browser. It includes:
- Unit contribution, margin, break-even and target-surplus calculator
- Simple cash runway estimator
- Stock reorder-point planner
- Receivables register with balance and overdue-age display
- Daily sales log with CSV export
- JSON backup and restore

**Privacy:** Your records stay in your browser's local storage. The app has no external scripts, analytics or network connection. Export backups regularly.

## What is included
- `index.html` — offline app
- Hindi user guide
- Future update notes when versioned improvements are released

## Who it is for
Micro and small business owners who want a lightweight first layer of operating visibility before adopting a larger ERP.

## Important limitations
This is not a full ERP, accounting or GST product. It does not issue tax invoices, calculate statutory taxes, connect to banks, sync across devices or provide professional advice. Results depend on user-entered data.
