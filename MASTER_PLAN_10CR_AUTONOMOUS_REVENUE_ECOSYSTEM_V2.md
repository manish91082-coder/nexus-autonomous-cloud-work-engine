## 3. Economic north star and accounting

Target: ₹10 crore cumulative realized net receipts.

`Realized net receipts = actual funds received and controlled - platform/payment fees - FX/withdrawal fees - refunds/reversals/chargebacks - attributable infrastructure/model/API/production costs`.

Track separately:
- discovered demand / listing value;
- gross checkout or sale;
- provider-cleared balance;
- payout initiated;
- bank/UPI/withdrawal settlement;
- refunds/reversals;
- actual costs;
- realized net receipts.

Only settled, controlled funds count toward the target. Downloads, views, followers, installs, listings, royalties accrued, escrow, accepted submissions and pending provider balances are not realized revenue. Tax obligations are recorded for owner review; no tax advice or liability assumption is invented.

## 5. Opportunity qualification: hard gates before score

An opportunity cannot be built merely because it is easy to code or a platform is popular.

### Hard gates (all must pass)
1. Real, specific user demand: official marketplace/search evidence, credible buyer language, observed conversion or other independently attributable signal.
2. Clear distribution: named platform/channel, discoverability mechanism and current eligibility.
3. India operator eligibility and payout path: method, minimum, fees, hold, settlement schedule, FX and owner KYC gate understood.
4. Automation fit: at least 90% of recurring workflow can be automated without policy bypass or human-dependent service delivery. The 90% is an internal selection threshold, not a claim about platform capability.
5. Zero-cost feasibility: no unapproved spending, paid API, mandatory bond, inventory or hidden recurring cost.
6. Positive expected unit economics after platform fees, refunds, support, FX, compute and production cost.
7. IP, licensing, privacy, security and platform policy compliance.
8. Objective QA and a measurable success/failure condition.
9. No owner-only legal, financial or identity action is silently taken.

Unknown = HOLD_FOR_EVIDENCE. Failed = REJECT. A high score cannot override a failed hard gate.

### Weighted score after hard gates
- Demand evidence: 25 points.
- Payout certainty and time-to-settlement: 20.
- End-to-end automation: 20.
- Net unit economics and zero-cost feasibility: 15.
- Distribution strength and competitive differentiation: 10.
- Policy, IP, security and operational risk: 10.

Decision bands:
- 75–100: eligible for a bounded pilot, subject to owner gates.
- 60–74: research/validation only; do not build full product.
- Below 60: reject/archive.
- Any failed hard gate: hold/reject regardless of score.

## 6. Standard opportunity-to-cash lifecycle

`DISCOVER → VERIFY DEMAND → VERIFY PLATFORM/INDIA PAYOUT → SCORE ECONOMICS → SELECT → BUILD MINIMUM ASSET → AUTOMATED QA → HUMAN/DEVICE PLAYTEST IF REQUIRED → PACKAGE → OWNER APPROVAL → PUBLISH → MEASURE → OPTIMIZE → PAYOUT → BANK RECONCILIATION`

Every opportunity gets a record containing:
- source URL, capture date/time and evidence;
- user problem and demand evidence;
- competitors/substitutes and differentiation;
- platform terms, country eligibility, payout route and delay;
- automation percentage and human gates;
- cost/fee model and expected net unit economics;
- score, decision and reason;
- build/test/commit/artifact references;
- publishing and settlement evidence.

No product build before the selection gate. No public submission before owner approval. No scaling before at least one genuine conversion and payout-path evidence.

## 7. One-time shared automation foundation

Build reusable modules once, then plug multiple revenue lanes into them.

1. **Control plane / project registry:** single mission config, constraints, source registry, decision register, active lane and phase state.
2. **Demand radar:** low-frequency, bounded, policy-compliant discovery of search/marketplace demand and competitor changes.
3. **Platform & payout verifier:** country eligibility, terms, KYC gate, fees, minimum, holds, settlement, refund rules and source freshness.
4. **Opportunity scoring engine:** hard gates + weighted score; explainable HOLD/REJECT/SELECT.
5. **Asset factory:** reusable content/code/data/game packaging; original-source and rights provenance.
6. **QA factory:** unit tests, browser tests, mobile viewports, accessibility, performance, security, originality, duplicate and file-integrity checks.
7. **Publishing adapters:** platform-specific packaging/metadata/SDK; no login or terms bypass; write operations stay approval-gated.
8. **Inbound discovery layer:** SEO metadata, platform search metadata, legitimate content distribution and analytics. No paid ads.
9. **Fulfilment/support automation:** only where platform permits and no unsafe autonomous commitments are made.
10. **Revenue ledger:** gross sale → provider-cleared → payout initiated → bank-settled; fees, FX, refunds and costs; evidence links.
11. **Operations dashboard:** channel status, next action, CI health, approvals required, cash and actual settled receipts.
12. **Continuity/recovery:** every chat resumes from GitHub master plan + latest checkpoint, not from conversational guesswork.
