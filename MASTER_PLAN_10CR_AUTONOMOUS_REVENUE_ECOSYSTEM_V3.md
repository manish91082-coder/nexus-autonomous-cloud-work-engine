## 1. Mission and operating interpretation

Build a diversified, Internet-only revenue portfolio targeting **₹10,00,00,000 in verified realized net receipts**. Starting capital is ₹0. The system must prefer work that can be configured once, then discovered, produced, delivered, distributed, measured and reconciled autonomously. A one-time human setup or approval does not disqualify an opportunity.

The target is an ambition, not a forecast or guarantee. No revenue estimate becomes a promise. The portfolio is judged by actual settled cash/controlled assets, net of costs, fees, refunds and reversals.

## 2. Expanded eligibility: one-time human setup, then automation

An opportunity is eligible when:
- its recurring workflow can be automated end-to-end to the maximum practical extent;
- human involvement is limited to legitimate one-time setup or exceptional owner approvals;
- recurring human labour, manual prospecting, manual fulfilment or manual customer service is not the business model;
- the platform/payment route is legal, available to an India-based operator and independently verified;
- it has real demand, defensible differentiation, positive net economics and acceptable IP/privacy/security risk.

Permitted one-time/exceptional human gates:
- identity/KYC and tax forms;
- account creation, ownership attestations and bank/wallet payout setup;
- accepting platform terms, licences and contracts;
- signing wallet transactions or authorising financial transfers;
- final approval of public releases, paid listings, irreversible publication or personal-data disclosure;
- device-specific checks that cannot be reliably simulated, and genuine dispute/escalation cases.

The AI system should prepare all forms, evidence, assets and step-by-step owner actions, then pause only at the exact gate. It must never impersonate the owner, bypass KYC, sign legal terms, use private keys/seed phrases, or spend funds without explicit approval.

## 3. Broad revenue universe (no single-channel restriction)

Research and qualify all lawful Internet-based methods where recurring work is automatable. This list is intentionally open, not a commitment to activate every category.

1. **Software and digital utilities:** browser apps, desktop/mobile utilities, plugins, extensions, APIs, micro-SaaS, workflow automation, developer tools, paid components and self-serve licences.
2. **Interactive products:** HTML5 games, mobile/desktop games, game assets, game templates, in-game/platform-native monetisation and licensing.
3. **Digital goods:** templates, spreadsheets, datasets, calculators, research packs, design systems, code libraries, original educational assets and downloadable tools.
4. **Digital publishing:** books, technical manuals, reference products, serial publications and platform-native royalties.
5. **Licensing and royalties:** original code, data, graphics, video, audio, 3D assets, fonts, models, stock media and reusable intellectual property.
6. **Affiliate and referral systems:** approved programs with trackable inbound/search/product-led acquisition and automated disclosure/compliance.
7. **Audience and platform monetisation:** video, newsletters, niche portals, educational channels, podcasts and platform-native advertising/revenue shares.
8. **Self-serve marketplaces:** paid listings, plugins, themes, extensions, integrations, app marketplaces, asset stores and creator stores.
9. **Data and information products:** lawful, licensed, privacy-safe datasets, indices, APIs, reports and machine-readable feeds.
10. **Automated transaction/licensing rails:** lawful on-chain software/asset licensing, smart-contract distribution, protocol incentives and direct wallet settlement where the activity and counterparty are compliant and independently verifiable.
11. **Other newly discovered models:** include any category that passes the same hard gates, even if not listed above.

Not eligible: jobs/employment, manual freelance bids or gigs, manual assignment labour, cold outreach, field/physical operations, installation/procurement/logistics, bespoke client service, recurring human fulfilment, spam, fake engagement, deceptive monetisation, copyright infringement, privacy violations, platform abuse or regulatory evasion. A category is not excluded merely because it is unfamiliar or pays in a different currency.

## 4. Payment acceptance policy: rail-neutral, compliance-first

We accept and evaluate every legitimate payout form that can be controlled and evidenced:
- Indian bank transfer, NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, UPI;
- international bank transfer/wire and local bank settlement;
- PayPal, Payoneer, Skrill and other platform-approved e-wallets;
- platform balances and marketplace payouts, subject to settlement/withdrawal rules;
- crypto assets/stablecoins paid directly to a wallet, where platform terms, Indian law, counterparty compliance, custody and conversion are verified;
- other lawful rails discovered during research.

A payment method is not considered usable merely because a wallet address or signup page exists. Verify country eligibility, KYC, provider licensing/registration where applicable, fees, minimums, lockups, reversibility, chargebacks, FX/conversion, tax records, custody and ability to use/convert the funds.

**Crypto controls:** record chain, transaction hash, asset, quantity, timestamp, wallet control evidence, fair INR valuation source/time, network fee, custody/exchange route and conversion evidence. Do not route funds through unregistered or sanctioned providers, mixers, concealment schemes or false jurisdictions. A wallet receipt is a distinct asset receipt, not automatically bank cash. Report it separately until its value, control and lawful usability are evidenced. Tax treatment of VDAs and transactions must be checked against current Indian law and reviewed by a qualified tax professional; do not assume that all crypto receipts have one uniform tax treatment.

## 5. Priority: fastest credible path to substantial receipts

Do not rank by headline earning claims, theoretical TAM or maximum possible revenue. Rank by **risk-adjusted time-to-first-settlement and scalable net receipts**.

### Priority tiers
- **P0 — Fastest verified settlement candidates:** platforms with proven India eligibility, existing buyer intent/discovery, low/no setup cost, clear payout rules and products/assets that can be built quickly. Run a broad source sweep, then launch the strongest 1–2 bounded pilots.
- **P1 — High-value scalable systems:** software licences, APIs, plugins, niche utilities, game distribution and self-serve products with credible recurring demand. Start only after a buyer/problem signal and payout route pass.
- **P2 — Portfolio compounding:** books, stock/licensing libraries, affiliate content and audience monetisation. These may have slower ramps and payout cycles; build only where repeatable demand and differentiated supply are evidenced.
- **P3 — Experimental/long-horizon:** on-chain monetisation, protocol rewards, novel marketplaces and speculative mechanisms. Research in parallel, but never count token emissions, points, airdrop rumours or unrealised balances as revenue.

### Scoring (100 points, after all hard gates pass)
- Verified demand and buyer intent: 25
- India eligibility and payout certainty/time: 20
- Recurring automation and human-independence: 20
- Risk-adjusted net economics and zero-upfront feasibility: 15
- Distribution strength and differentiation: 10
- Legal, IP, privacy, security and platform risk: 10

Decision: 80+ pilot priority; 70–79 eligible bounded pilot; 55–69 validation only; below 55 reject/archive. Hard-gate failure overrides score. Where settlement speed and large revenue potential conflict, maintain separate scores and portfolio lanes rather than hiding the trade-off.

### Required evidence for every candidate
Official platform terms; country/creator eligibility; payout methods and thresholds; hold/settlement schedule; fees/FX/refunds; demand evidence; competition; monetisation mechanics; AI-content rules; rights/licences; expected recurring human work; infrastructure requirements; realistic unit economics; date-stamped source links. Unknowns remain HOLD, never assumed true.

## 6. Cash accounting and performance

`Realized net receipts = settled bank/UPI/e-wallet funds + controlled, independently valued digital assets accepted under the accounting policy - platform/payment/network/FX fees - refunds/reversals/chargebacks - attributable operating costs.`

Maintain separate ledgers:
1. gross orders/sales;
2. provider-cleared balance;
3. payout initiated;
4. settled fiat;
5. wallet receipts (unconverted);
6. converted/withdrawn digital assets;
7. fees, taxes withheld, refunds and operating costs;
8. cumulative realized net receipts in INR.

No views, installs, clicks, leads, listings, pending royalties, points, airdrop eligibility, escrow, token promises or unwithdrawable balances count. Never double-count the same asset when received and later converted.

Build the common system once and connect revenue lanes through adapters.

- **Mission control plane:** canonical config, phase, constraints, risk limits, owner gates and execution state.
- **Opportunity radar:** multi-source discovery across marketplaces, app stores, search demand, creator platforms, affiliate networks, developer ecosystems and lawful on-chain ecosystems.
- **Evidence vault:** immutable source URL, capture timestamp, quote/snippet, hash, evidence freshness, claims supported and contradiction log.
- **Payout and eligibility registry:** country, entity type, KYC, rails, thresholds, holds, fees, tax forms, FX and settlement evidence.
- **Opportunity ranker:** hard gates, tier, score, expected time-to-first-sale and time-to-settlement, risk-adjusted upside, confidence and stop conditions.
- **Asset/product factory:** original research, code, content, media, metadata, packaging, versioning and provenance.
- **Quality and policy factory:** automated tests, browser/mobile QA, accessibility, security, licensing, originality, platform rules and content disclosures.
- **Distribution adapters:** marketplace metadata, store packaging, compliant publishing, version updates and analytics. Public/financial/contractual actions remain approval-gated.
- **Fulfilment layer:** automated delivery, entitlement, licence issuance, receipts, updates and bounded support where permitted.
- **Growth loop:** product-led discovery, SEO, platform-native metadata, legitimate referral links, conversion experiments and retention. No paid ads or fake traffic.
- **Revenue ledger:** provider balance, payouts, fiat and digital-asset receipts, fees, refunds and reconciliation.
- **Operations dashboard:** current lane, next task, blockers, owner gates, CI, source freshness, cash and actual receipts.
- **Recovery and continuity:** persistent task queue, idempotency, leases, checkpoints, retry/backoff, dead-letter queue, resumable phase state and cross-thread handoff.

## 8. Autonomous operating loop (no repeated “next” prompts)

The target operating mode is **continuous execution with explicit stop gates**, not a chat that waits for a user to type “next”.

1. At each scheduled run, read the canonical plan, state, queue, evidence and latest CI.
2. Reconcile actual GitHub HEAD and last successful run before acting.
3. Select the highest-priority unblocked task by score, dependency and expected value.
4. Execute one bounded, reversible task; parallelise independent research only when it does not create conflicting writes.
5. Run tests, validate source freshness and inspect diffs.
6. Commit code/docs/evidence; update task state and checkpoint atomically where possible.
7. If failed, capture logs, classify transient/permanent, retry safely with bounded exponential backoff, or move to dead-letter/HOLD.
8. If owner-only action is required, prepare a concise approval packet and pause only that task; continue unrelated safe tasks.
9. Produce a durable session report with completed work, evidence, commit, CI, receipts and next task.
10. On a new ChatGPT thread, resume from repository state; never restart or ask for context already stored.

**Important platform limitation:** GitHub Actions can run deterministic scripts/tests on a schedule; it is not itself an autonomous reasoning model and cannot perform arbitrary research or sign in to third-party accounts without explicitly configured capabilities/secrets. Scheduled ChatGPT task automation can initiate research/execution sessions, but it is not a 24/7 daemon and cannot bypass account approval gates. Use both layers: scheduled agent sessions for reasoning and GitHub Actions for deterministic checks. If a scheduled agent run is unavailable or fails, the durable queue remains resumable on the next user session.

The repository, not the chat transcript, is the memory authority. Maintain:

- `control/opportunity_registry.json`: all candidates, sources, scores and decisions;
- `control/payout_registry.json`: payment rails and India eligibility evidence;
- `control/revenue_ledger.json`: verified receipts only;
- `research/...`: dated evidence reports;
- `logs/...`: run reports, test summaries and failures.

Every thread must begin with: read v3 master, continuity, machine state, queue, latest report, GitHub HEAD and latest workflow run. The first response should resume the highest-priority unfinished task, not repeat planning. If a chat approaches its limit, write a handoff checkpoint containing exact task ID, files, commit SHA, verified evidence, unresolved issues and next action. The new thread needs only the repository URL and instruction: “₹10 Cr mission resume; read V3 continuity and execute the next queued task.”

## 10. Execution safeguards

- ₹0 default spend. No paid plan/API/cloud/ads/credits/asset purchases, deposits or subscriptions without explicit approval.
- Never store secrets, passwords, OTPs, private keys, seed phrases, wallet recovery phrases or KYC documents in Git.
- Use least-privilege tokens; never print secrets in logs.
- No uncontrolled auto-publication, financial transfers, wallet signing, contract acceptance, KYC, account ownership or tax declarations.
- Use idempotent actions and bounded retries; never duplicate listings, payments or submissions.
- Keep at most two active build pilots; discovery can be broad.
- Stop work that has no new evidence, fails payout eligibility, requires ongoing manual work or has negative economics.
- Preserve legacy history; label it rather than mass-deleting.
