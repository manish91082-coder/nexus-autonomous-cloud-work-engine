## 2. Revenue recognition: platform-wallet-first, cash-settlement separate

The owner has explicitly directed that a real earning credited to an eligible platform seller/creator balance may count toward the mission before bank withdrawal. Therefore the mission will not incorrectly treat bank withdrawal as the only revenue milestone.

### Primary KPI: Platform-Credited Net Earnings (PCNE)

Count an amount when all of the following are true:

1. A genuine independent customer, licensee, sponsor, or buyer has paid for the product/use.
2. The platform's seller/creator dashboard or authoritative transaction statement records the owner's earning as credited/earned.
3. Platform commission, marketplace share, payment processing fees, direct usage/compute/proxy/storage charges, refunds, reversals and chargebacks attributable to the transaction have been deducted or separately reserved.
4. The amount is not a bonus, grant, promotional credit, free-plan allowance, self-purchase, test transaction, coupon subsidy, pending card authorization, or forecast.
5. The balance is under the owner's account/control and is not shown as disputed, reversible, suspended, or forfeitable. If KYC or another platform condition makes it forfeitable, it is reported separately until the condition is satisfied.

### Separate financial states

- `gross_customer_sales`: what independent buyers paid.
- `platform_credited_net_earnings`: primary mission progress after known platform and direct usage deductions.
- `restricted_or_held_earnings`: credited but not currently available, under review, KYC-held or exposed to forfeiture; excluded from PCNE until cleared.
- `withdrawable_platform_balance`: currently available amount in the provider dashboard.
- `bank_settled_net_receipts`: money actually received in the owner's bank/wallet after withdrawal.
- `refunds_reversals_and_costs`: separately reconciled adjustments.

No amount may be counted twice when it moves from platform balance to bank account. Currency conversion into INR must retain the source rate and timestamp. The mission's primary progress is PCNE; bank settlement remains a secondary realization and liquidity metric.

**Tax optimisation, payout-method optimisation, FX routing and bank settlement architecture are deferred workstreams**, as instructed. Deferral does not authorize false seller identity, restricted-country evasion, bypassing mandatory platform KYC/terms, hiding funds, or publishing where the platform requires an owner action that has not occurred.

## 3. What we will build and what we will not build

### Eligible revenue models

- Paid SaaS and marketplace apps with recurring subscriptions or usage billing.
- AI apps/agents and developer/business utilities.
- Automation products and APIs with self-serve onboarding and usage-based revenue.
- Digital tools, templates, downloadable software and licensed IP where real buyer demand is evidenced.
- Game/software marketplace revenue where platform acceptance and organic distribution are demonstrated.
- Open-source products with a credible paid layer or voluntary sponsorship adoption.

### Excluded as the core business model

- Employment, job offers, manual gigs, freelance bidding, manual assignment labour.
- Cold outreach, spam, fake reviews, fake traffic, self-purchases or fabricated customers.
- Recurring human implementation, bespoke consulting, field operations or manual fulfilment as the revenue engine.
- Unauthorized scraping, resale of restricted data, copyright misuse, privacy violations, CAPTCHA/access-control bypass, or platform-policy evasion.
- Building infrastructure, features, agents or dashboards that have no tested connection to paid buyer demand.

## 4. Priority principle: largest credible scalable revenue first

Revenue lanes are ranked by risk-adjusted scalable revenue potential, not by headline market size alone.

Required dimensions:
1. Proven buyer activity and evidence of money changing hands.
2. Average recurring value per paying customer or unit.
3. Marketplace discovery and reach.
4. Repeat purchase/retention potential.
5. Automation and self-serve delivery.
6. Net economics after platform share and runtime costs.
7. Zero-upfront feasibility and time to first credited earnings.
8. Platform, source-rights, security and operational risk.

An attractive platform is not yet a product. A market-size statistic is not product demand. A successful CI run is not buyer demand. A listing is not a sale.

The dated ranking and source adjudication lives in `REVENUE_OPPORTUNITY_RANKING_2026-11-13.md`. The ranking is rechecked after each meaningful evidence batch.

## 6. Product selection and release gates

A product may enter build only when its product dossier records:

- Exact buyer persona, job-to-be-done and buying trigger.
- Primary-source evidence of platform capability and terms.
- Marketplace competitors: listing date, pricing model, installs/users/reviews where shown, update cadence, feature coverage and gaps.
- At least two independent demand signals. Strong signals include public buyer complaints, feature requests, paid competitor activity, marketplace transactions or recurring operational constraints. Likes, compliments and generic market-size numbers are weak signals.
- A defensible gap not already served by a free incumbent.
- A measurable customer outcome and unit economics.
- Rights to every data source, dependency, model and asset.
- A self-serve onboarding, delivery, support and update plan.
- Security, privacy, access-scope and failure-mode review.
- Deterministic tests and reproducible quality evidence.

Release sequence:
`RESEARCH → COMPETITOR AUDIT → BUYER GAP → PRODUCT SPEC → MVP → DETERMINISTIC TESTS → SECURITY/PRIVACY QA → PRIVATE PACKAGE → OWNER/PLATFORM GATES → PUBLISH → MEASURE WALLET EARNINGS → OPTIMISE OR KILL`

A product is not marked LIVE until the public listing is verified. A product is not marked PAID_USE until an independent paid order is evidenced. A product is not marked REVENUE until PCNE is evidenced.
